# Demo walkthrough

Use the production preview from [handoff](handoff.md). A fresh anonymous browser is useful for a clean walkthrough; keep an existing learner's history unless they choose to delete it.

## Read and improve

1. Choose English and ask to understand photosynthesis, distinguishing materials from sunlight's energy. Use live generation.
2. Answer a baseline question with "I don't know yet", then skip the rest. The concept must remain unknown.
3. Read the sourced lesson, its visual and glossary. Opening it must not establish understanding. Ask the contextual tutor a question about a source-supported idea.
4. In Explain Back, deliberately say sunlight becomes the material in sugar. Answer the neutral follow-up with the same misconception. Inspect the saved quoted findings, repair and fresh application.
5. Explain again in your own words after reading the repair. State which materials enter and which products leave, and distinguish energy from matter. Answer the new application's actual conditions. Demonstrated understanding requires every essential idea and correct application evidence.
6. Open progress, expand both attempts and reload. The old misconception remains beside the later improvement.

The intentional answers above are synthetic demo input, not a learner assessment recommendation.

## Broader objective and languages

Ask to learn photosynthesis and how carbon moves through living things. Inspect its bounded pathway and first concept, then reload. Switch between English, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo through settings and prepare each content version. Progress persists; saved learner words retain their original language. Read the actual capability matrix rather than implying equal speech quality.

## Listen, watch and save

Prepare audio explicitly. Read its transcript, then play or download it. Prepare video, open the player, turn on captions and compare its transcript/source IDs with the lesson. No audio or video should download automatically in low-data mode. English and Hausa have reviewed local examples. Yoruba/Igbo audio remains experimental and may be withheld after review.

Choose Save text for offline, open Saved and read the copy. Reload with the web server unavailable after the shell was cached. Download the lesson text and inspect its source URLs. Tutoring, assessments, generation and media still require a connection.

## Voice and device acceptance

With available session quota and a working microphone, open Talk with òye., start voice and ask a short source-supported question. Check the signal meter, saved recognized words, reviewed reply playback, Mute, Interrupt reply, Stop and reconnect. Confirm spoken Explain Back words before submitting. A saved recording can exercise the same path when the microphone is broken, but does not prove live capture.

On a physical phone, verify the software keyboard leaves the composer/submit button reachable. Deny microphone permission, then check typing and audio upload. In a real screen reader, navigate the home, assessment, lesson, tutor, Explain Back, progress, settings and saved-text controls. Check labels, focus order, live errors, dialog focus and text alternatives. Record device, browser, screen reader and outcome. Responsive desktop snapshots and accessibility trees provide separate, narrower evidence.

## Failure and privacy checks

Observe a slow or failed job's saved input and retry/cancel action; text remains usable after media failure. Open an owned API resource from a second anonymous session and expect 404. Clear only a disposable walkthrough owner's history, confirm that its learning records and files disappear, and confirm global usage reservations remain. Do not clear a real learner's history for a demonstration without their choice.
