Checked 13 October 2026. [Stored live evidence](m09-live.json) contains model IDs, accepted narration reviews, recording receipts and evaluator links. Inputs were synthetic acceptance samples. These checks do not certify native pronunciation or recognition of human accents.

## Confirmed speech evidence

English, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo synthetic recordings were recognized, corrected explicitly to the intended acceptance explanation, confirmed, and sent through the existing Explain Back evaluator. All four reached a saved neutral follow-up. This verifies routing and preservation of evidence, rather than claiming a human independently demonstrated understanding through these synthetic inputs.

The native browser uploaded the Yoruba generated sample, displayed recognition with spelling errors, accepted a correction to the public question's intended products, required a matching radio choice, and saved it. Reload retained the selection. The API now returns the matching receipt's original recognition, confirmed words and original language, so both remain inspectable. Repeating an identical saved selection is idempotent. Changing already used words is rejected.

## Recording and failures

The in-app microphone request initially remained pending. A 16-second deadline returned a translated text/upload fallback with enabled controls. After the user's request to retry, real capture started, the timer advanced, Stop produced a local clip, and Discard removed it. That microphone clip was not submitted for transcription.

The requested denial check also exercised the actual microphone helper with a rejected `NotAllowedError`. Four focused checks passed for denial fallback, granted capture, cancellation followed by a late grant, and timeout followed by a late grant. Late microphone tracks stopped. This is a mocked permission rejection with native timeout/capture checks, not a claim that a browser Deny button was clicked. Physical-phone microphone and human accent review remain M12 checks.

HTTP acceptance checks passed for oversized bodies at 413, uploaded playlist rejection at 422, corrupt audio rejection at 422, recordings over 90 seconds at 422, foreign origin at 403, owner isolation at 404, unconfirmed and wrong-context receipts at 409, identical request reuse, confirmed selection persistence, identical answer save reuse, and immutable used words. Narration requests reused the same job and byte ranges returned 206. Deleting a disposable session removed its receipts and audio from the API and its generated file from disk.

Uploads are decoded only as allowlisted audio containers into generated local paths. Temporary files are removed in `finally` and at server startup. Recognized and confirmed words remain until history deletion or session-retention cleanup. Provider-side retention is controlled by the selected provider. The app does not log raw recordings, credentials or provider error objects.
