# Adaptive Learning Companion
### Product Overview

**Learning that adapts to you, not the other way around.**

## 1. What We're Building

An intelligent, adaptive learning companion that makes quality education accessible to anyone, regardless of their existing knowledge, language, literacy level, abilities, or access to technology.

Instead of expecting learners to find resources, follow fixed courses, or learn in a particular way, the platform understands **what each person wants to learn, what they already know, and how they can best engage with learning.**

It then creates personalized, interactive learning experiences using short explanations, AI-generated video explainers, audio lessons, illustrations, real-world examples, case studies, and practice questions, all grounded in credible educational sources.

Learners also have a conversational AI tutor they can speak with naturally or chat with through text. They can ask questions, request explanations, discuss concepts, and teach what they've learned back to the AI to identify gaps in their understanding.

The platform continuously adapts based on what learners understand, where they struggle, and how their needs change.

**Our core belief: Learning should adapt to the learner, not require the learner to adapt to the platform.**

## 2. The Problem

Access to educational content does not necessarily mean access to effective learning.

Learners, particularly across Africa, face several challenges:

- **Scattered resources:** Quality materials are spread across websites, videos, textbooks, and learning platforms, making them difficult to discover and organize.
- **One-size-fits-all learning:** Traditional courses often assume learners have similar backgrounds, knowledge, and learning needs.
- **Language barriers:** Much of the available educational content is not accessible in learners' preferred or native languages.
- **Literacy and accessibility barriers:** Text-heavy learning platforms exclude or disadvantage people with limited literacy or certain disabilities.
- **Passive learning:** Watching videos and reading materials doesn't necessarily translate into understanding or retention.
- **Limited support:** Learners often have nobody to ask when they encounter difficult concepts.
- **Poor learning continuity:** Learners become overwhelmed, lose motivation, or abandon learning when they struggle.
- **Connectivity and device constraints:** Expensive mobile data, unreliable internet, and limited access to capable devices restrict participation.

The problem isn't simply a shortage of information. It's that **available information is often not delivered in a form that individual learners can access, understand, engage with, and benefit from.**

## 3. Our Solution

We create a personalized learning experience around the individual.

The platform brings together five capabilities:

### 1. Adaptive Learning

The platform assesses a learner's existing knowledge before deciding what to teach.

It generates learning experiences tailored to their understanding, pace, objectives, and knowledge gaps, rather than presenting everyone with the same content.

### 2. Multimodal, Accessible Learning

Learning materials can be delivered through different formats:

- Short, illustrated text lessons
- AI-generated video explainers
- Narrated audio lessons
- Interactive diagrams and visualizations
- Real-world examples and case studies
- Questions, answers, and practical exercises

The platform selects suitable formats based on the learner's preferences, literacy, accessibility requirements, device, and connectivity.

Learners can switch formats whenever they want.

### 3. Multilingual Learning

Learners can study and interact with their AI tutor in their preferred supported language.

Learning materials, video narration, questions, audio explanations, and tutor conversations can be localized.

The long-term goal is broad multilingual support, including African languages often underserved by conventional educational platforms.

### 4. Conversational AI Tutoring

Every learner has a personal AI tutor available through text and natural voice-to-voice conversations.

The tutor understands the learner's objectives, current materials, existing knowledge, and progress.

Learners can ask questions, request simpler explanations, explore examples, and discuss difficult concepts.

### 5. Active Understanding and Improvement

Learning doesn't stop after consuming content.

Learners explain concepts back to the AI, answer follow-up questions, and complete short assessments.

The AI identifies misconceptions, highlights missing knowledge, and creates targeted learning materials to help them improve.

This creates a continuous cycle of learning, feedback, and improvement.

## 4. How It Works

### Step 1: Tell AI What You Want to Learn

Learners begin by describing what they want to learn or achieve, through text or voice.

The platform supports three types of learning objectives:

**Goals — Something the learner wants to achieve**

Examples:
- "I want to prepare for WAEC Biology."
- "I want to learn how to build websites."
- "I want to improve my English communication."

**Topics — A subject the learner wants to understand**

Examples:
- "Teach me photosynthesis."
- "I want to understand monetary policy."
- "I want to learn basic algebra."

**Concepts — A specific idea or question**

Examples:
- "How does compound interest work?"
- "What is chlorophyll?"
- "Why does the moon orbit the Earth?"

Learners don't need to categorize their requests. The AI identifies their scope and creates an appropriate learning experience.

### Step 2: Understand the Learner

The platform considers two things.

**A. Learner preferences and constraints**

These may include:

- Preferred language
- Preferred content format
- Reading and literacy needs
- Accessibility requirements
- Device and connectivity limitations
- Available learning time

Learners can provide these preferences during onboarding and modify them later.

**B. Existing knowledge**

Before generating learning materials, the AI asks a few simple multiple-choice questions to gauge the learner's familiarity with the subject and identify potential knowledge gaps.

The assessment adapts to the learner's needs. For example, questions can be spoken aloud, with answers given through voice rather than text.

Learners can indicate when they don't know an answer. The assessment is intended to guide teaching, not exclude anyone.

### Step 3: Structure the Learning Journey

The AI organizes the learning objective according to its scope.

**Goal → Topics → Concepts → Learning Experiences**

For example:

**Goal: Prepare for WAEC Biology**

- Topic: Cell Biology
  - Concept: Cell structure
  - Concept: Cell division
- Topic: Genetics
  - Concept: Inheritance
  - Concept: Genes and chromosomes
- Topic: Ecology
  - Concept: Ecosystems
  - Concept: Food chains

The pathway is grounded in relevant curricula or credible source materials where applicable.

For a simple question or concept, the AI can generate one short learning experience without creating an unnecessary pathway.

**The concept, or a small group of related concepts, is the fundamental unit of learning.**

Goals organize the broader journey, topics organize the knowledge, and concepts provide manageable learning experiences.

The pathway can be adjusted as the learner progresses.

### Step 4: Generate Personalized Learning Experiences

The AI discovers relevant educational resources and uses them to create short, engaging learning materials suited to the learner.

A learning experience may include:

**Short explanations**

Concise, understandable text broken into manageable sections rather than lengthy traditional courses.

**Video explainers**

Short, AI-generated visual explanations with narration in the learner's preferred language. These may use illustrations, animations, diagrams, and examples to explain concepts.

**Audio lessons**

Narrated explanations for learners who prefer listening or need an audio-first experience.

**Visual explanations**

Diagrams, charts, illustrations, and interactive visualizations that help learners understand complex concepts.

**Examples and case studies**

Practical scenarios, including locally relevant examples, that demonstrate how knowledge applies to everyday life.

**Questions and answers**

Short quizzes and interactive questions embedded throughout the learning experience.

**Sources and further reading**

Links and references to credible materials, allowing learners to explore original sources and verify information.

Not every learner receives every format. The experience is assembled based on their needs and preferences.

For instance, a learner who cannot read may receive primarily narrated video explanations with spoken questions and answers, rather than a text-heavy lesson.

### Step 5: Learn With the AI Tutor

The tutor is available throughout the learning journey.

Learners can engage in voice-to-voice or text conversations to:

- Ask questions about what they're learning
- Request simpler or more detailed explanations
- Ask for additional examples
- Explore related concepts
- Request explanations in another language
- Ask for a visual illustration
- Discuss difficult ideas
- Get help with practice questions

The tutor shares context with the learning experience, so learners don't have to repeatedly explain what they're studying.

The tutor can also be accessed independently. Someone can ask a question without first creating a learning goal or generating a lesson.

If they want to explore the topic further, the conversation can become a new learning experience.

### Step 6: Explain What You've Learned

After learning, users can enter **Explain Back** mode.

In this mode, the learner becomes the teacher.

They explain a concept to the AI in their own words, using voice or text.

The AI:

1. Listens to or reads their explanation.
2. Identifies which ideas were explained correctly.
3. Detects possible misconceptions and missing concepts.
4. Asks follow-up questions to explore their understanding.
5. Provides constructive feedback.

Unlike regular tutoring, Explain Back focuses on assessing what the learner can explain independently, rather than immediately supplying answers.

### Step 7: Improve Through Targeted Learning

Based on knowledge checks, tutor interactions, and Explain Back sessions, the platform identifies areas requiring further attention.

Instead of repeating the entire lesson, it generates focused explanations, additional examples, practice questions, or video explainers addressing those specific gaps.

Learners can revisit the material and explain the concept again.

As understanding improves, the platform updates the learner's progress and recommends what to learn next.

### Step 8: Track Progress and Continue Learning

Learners have a persistent record of their learning journeys.

They can see:

- Learning goals and topics they're pursuing
- Concepts they've covered
- Areas where they've demonstrated understanding
- Concepts requiring further practice
- Previously generated learning materials
- Recommended next steps

They can resume learning from where they stopped, revisit earlier concepts, or change their learning objectives.

For longer-term goals, the platform helps learners see how individual concepts contribute to their broader objectives.

## 5. Personalized Learning in Practice

Consider three learners who want to understand how the human heart works.

### Learner A: Prefers Reading

The platform generates:

- Short illustrated explanations
- Labeled heart diagrams
- Interactive visualizations
- Practice questions
- Further reading

The learner can ask the AI tutor questions whenever they encounter something difficult.

### Learner B: Limited Reading Ability, Prefers Yoruba

The platform generates:

- Narrated video explainers in Yoruba
- Simple animations
- Familiar, locally relevant examples
- Spoken questions and answers
- Voice-to-voice tutoring in Yoruba

The learner can engage without needing to read lengthy educational materials.

### Learner C: Blind, Prefers Audio

The platform provides:

- Audio-first explanations
- Spoken descriptions of structures and processes
- Voice-based questions and assessments
- Conversational tutoring
- Navigation compatible with accessible input methods and assistive technology

Visual information is explained in words rather than being the only way to understand a concept.

**All three learners pursue the same objective, but receive different learning experiences designed around their individual needs.**

These are examples, not permanent learner categories. Each person controls their own preferences and can switch between formats.

## 6. The Core Learning Experience

The product follows one continuous learning loop:

**Discover → Learn → Ask → Explain → Improve**

**Discover:** Define an objective, assess existing knowledge, and retrieve relevant educational resources.

**Learn:** Engage with personalized explanations, videos, audio, illustrations, case studies, and questions.

**Ask:** Have natural conversations with a contextual AI tutor whenever help is needed.

**Explain:** Teach concepts back to the AI to demonstrate understanding.

**Improve:** Receive feedback and targeted learning materials, then continue progressing.

This loop repeats throughout the learning journey, adapting to the learner's changing knowledge and needs.

## 7. What Makes It Different

**Most learning platforms personalize what people learn. We personalize how they learn, too.**

Our differentiation comes from combining:

**Learner-first adaptation**

The platform considers knowledge, language, literacy, accessibility, preferred learning formats, connectivity, and individual circumstances—not just the topic requested.

**Learning experiences generated on demand**

Instead of forcing learners through a fixed library of courses, the platform assembles focused learning experiences around their objectives and actual knowledge gaps.

**A contextual, multilingual AI tutor**

Learners can have natural conversations in their preferred supported language, with a tutor that understands their learning context and progress.

**Active understanding rather than passive consumption**

The Explain Back experience encourages learners to articulate what they know, identify misunderstandings, and improve.

**Continuously adapting learning**

Learning materials, assessments, and next steps evolve based on demonstrated understanding instead of relying solely on completed lessons or time spent studying.

**Accessible learning by design**

The platform can create reading-based, video-first, audio-first, or mixed experiences so that literacy, disability, language, and device limitations don't unnecessarily prevent participation.

## 8. Accessibility, Quality, and Operating Constraints

The platform is designed with the realities of African learners in mind.

### Quality and Credibility

Learning content should be grounded in trustworthy educational sources, including official curricula, textbooks, open educational resources, academic institutions, and reputable publications.

References remain traceable, and AI-generated explanations should be checked against retrieved sources.

Source credibility and content accuracy must be maintained across translations and generated formats.

### Low Bandwidth and Limited Connectivity

The platform should support lightweight text lessons, compressed media, downloadable materials, and offline access to previously generated learning experiences where possible.

Users should be able to choose lower-data formats, and voice or video should not be required to access learning.

### Accessibility and Inclusion

The experience should support screen readers, accessible navigation, captions, transcripts, voice interaction, and alternative assessment methods.

Accessibility preferences should be user-controlled rather than inferred solely from a disability.

### Multilingual Access

The goal is to support learning in a wide range of languages, including African local languages.

Speech recognition, translation, narration, and tutoring quality must be evaluated for each supported language, with support expanded progressively.

### Personalization Without Exclusion

Knowledge assessments are used to decide where teaching should begin, not whether someone is allowed to learn.

Learners can revisit foundational concepts, skip familiar material, change formats, and progress at their own pace.

### Privacy and Security

Learning profiles, voice recordings, conversations, and assessment results should be protected.

The platform should minimize unnecessary personal data collection, provide clear retention controls, and allow learners to manage their learning history.

### Local Relevance

Examples, case studies, languages, terminology, and learning pathways should reflect learners' contexts.

Where appropriate, learning objectives can be aligned with local educational curricula and qualifications.

## 9. Product Structure

The learner-facing product has three primary areas.

### My Learning

The home for the learner's objectives and generated learning experiences.

Includes learning pathways, topics, concepts, lessons, videos, audio, exercises, and saved resources.

### AI Tutor

A conversational learning companion supporting text and voice-to-voice interactions.

Includes general Q&A, contextual lesson support, and Explain Back sessions.

The tutor is accessible independently and from within any learning experience.

### My Progress

A record of completed activities, demonstrated understanding, unresolved knowledge gaps, and recommended next steps.

Learners can revisit previous materials or resume interrupted learning journeys.

Language, accessibility, content-format, and connectivity preferences are available through settings and can be changed during learning.

## 10. Capstone MVP Scope

The capstone will demonstrate the core adaptive learning experience rather than attempt to build a production-ready learning platform.

### Core MVP

1. **Learning objective input:** Accept goals, topics, or individual concepts through a simple interface.
2. **Knowledge assessment:** Generate a short multiple-choice assessment to estimate current understanding.
3. **Learning structure:** Break broad goals into topics and concepts, or generate a focused experience for a single concept.
4. **Personalized content generation:** Produce concise lessons, visual explanations, real-world examples, practice questions, and credible source references.
5. **Video explainers:** Produce short narrated visual explainers from source-grounded scripts and simple visuals.
6. **Multilingual delivery:** Demonstrate content and tutoring in a small set of supported languages.
7. **AI Tutor:** Support contextual text and voice conversations for questions and explanations.
8. **Explain Back:** Accept spoken explanations, identify misconceptions, and provide follow-up questions and feedback.
9. **Adaptive improvement:** Generate targeted follow-up learning materials based on assessment results.
10. **Progress tracking:** Save learning experiences, assessment outcomes, and progress so users can resume.

### Demonstrating Inclusion

The prototype should demonstrate how the same objective can be delivered through at least two different learning formats, such as illustrated text and narrated video or audio.

It should also show how language and knowledge level affect the generated content.

For the sprint, a limited number of well-tested language and accessibility experiences is preferable to claiming universal support without reliable implementation.

### Outside Initial Scope

A full educational content marketplace, formal certifications, employment placement, extensive curriculum partnerships, and production-scale video generation infrastructure are not necessary for the capstone.

## 11. How We'll Measure Success

The proof of concept should demonstrate that learners can:

- Start learning from a goal, topic, or question without manually searching through numerous resources.
- Receive materials appropriate to their demonstrated knowledge and selected accessibility needs.
- Access explanations and interact with a tutor in supported languages.
- Engage with short learning activities and ask questions when confused.
- Explain a concept, receive meaningful feedback, and demonstrate improved understanding through follow-up assessments.
- Return to an existing learning experience and continue from where they stopped.

The most valuable demonstration would compare a learner's understanding before and after a personalized learning experience, while showing how the platform adapted its explanations and formats along the way.

## 12. Alignment With the Capstone Challenge

**Track 1 — Access & Discovery of Learning Resources**

The platform automatically discovers credible sources and transforms scattered learning materials into accessible, organized experiences tailored to each learner.

**Track 2 — Engagement & Staying in the Learning Journey**

Interactive materials, voice conversations, Teachback, feedback, targeted practice, and resumable learning journeys help learners remain actively involved.

**Track 3 — Guidance, Pathways & Opportunity**

Learners can begin with broader objectives and receive structured pathways showing what to learn, what they've understood, and what comes next. This helps connect individual learning activities to real educational and personal goals.

The solution spans all three tracks, with its strongest emphasis on making quality learning accessible, personally relevant, and engaging.

## 13. Vision

We envision a future where anyone can have a personal learning companion that understands them.

A learner shouldn't need to speak English fluently, read complex textbooks, afford expensive courses, own a powerful device, or have the same educational background as everyone else to access meaningful learning.

They should be able to express what they want to understand, in a language and format comfortable to them, and receive an experience that helps them genuinely learn.

**Our goal isn't simply to make more educational content available. It's to make learning understandable, accessible, engaging, and effective for each individual.**

### Core Value Proposition

**Quality learning, in a way that works for you.**

A learning companion that understands what you know, how you communicate, and what you need—then adapts how it teaches, answers your questions, and helps you improve.
