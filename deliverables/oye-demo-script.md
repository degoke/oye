## Narration

### 0:00.000 · story

> Imagine Roliat. Her family has moved from a village to a town, and she is returning to school after interrupted education. She speaks Yoruba confidently. She is still learning to read. She has watched vegetables grow at home. Now her teacher asks her to explain photosynthesis. The textbook is in English. Roliat looks down at the page and stays quiet. She is curious. She needs a way into the lesson. òye. starts with what she wants to understand, in a language she understands.

### 0:34.000 · english

> This is òye. Learning that understands you. We will start in English so you can follow the learning loop. A photosynthesis request creates a learning journey. The app asks about your starting point, then opens a sourced lesson. The lesson explains the process, gives a visual summary, and keeps the source visible.

### 1:00.000 · learning

> Now the important part. The learner says that sunlight becomes the material in sugar. òye. asks a follow-up before giving corrective feedback. It checks what the learner means, then identifies the misconception using words from the answer. The repair separates materials from energy. Carbon dioxide and water are materials used. Light supplies energy. Carbohydrates and oxygen are products. A later explanation and a fresh application check whether the learner can use that distinction. The new answer correctly treats sunlight as energy, rather than sugar material. Both attempts stay saved. Progress changes because of the evidence. Watching a video alone does not establish understanding.

### 1:55.000 · yoruba intro

> Now let us learn in Yoruba. A teacher helps Roliat begin. She can start by speaking her question.

### 2:12.216 · yoruba bridge

> òye. recognizes the request. Here it is reading a starting question in Yoruba.

### 2:29.216 · yoruba course

> Her course includes explanations, a diagram and sources. Here is a video. The explanation is in Yoruba too.

### 3:00.216 · yoruba close

> Learners can choose English, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo for their lessons. Save text for later, and keep learning when the connection drops.

### 3:12.000 · codex

> Codex worked through this project from the beginning. AGENTS.md records our stack and working rules. The plan divides the build into small milestones. Decisions document what changed and why. During review, we found that clearing history could reset usage limits. Codex implemented retained counters and checked them separately. The Agents SDK runs the app's AI. Codex powered development. Setup, architecture and deployment notes give another developer a place to start.

### 3:46.000 · closing

> With another month, we would pilot with learners and teachers, measure how misconceptions improve, refine translations and deploy a hosted pilot. For Roliat, the aim is concrete. Hear an idea. Make sense of it. Explain it in her own words. òye. Learn anything. In your language. In your way.
